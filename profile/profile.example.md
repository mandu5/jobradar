# Candidate profile (example)

Copy this to `profile/profile.md` and rewrite it as yourself. The scorer reads this file and
`rubric.md` and nothing else about you, so anything you leave vague here comes back as a vague
grade. Keep it under ~60 lines: it is pasted into every scoring run.

`profile/profile.md` is gitignored by default. Keep it that way if your fork is public.

## Basics

- Name, city, commute radius. Whether you can relocate.
- Degree, school, graduation date, GPA if it helps you.
- Work authorization / visa status, and military service if your market asks for it.
- Languages, with test scores if you have them.

## Experience

- One line per role: employer / title / dates / what you actually shipped, with numbers.
- State plainly how much experience counts as "years" on a job posting. This is the single
  most useful line in the file — it decides how the hard filters fire.
  e.g. "New grad. Postings asking 3+ years are out; 'entry level or equivalent' is in."

## Skills

- What you are strong at, and — just as important — **what you are not**. The scorer will
  otherwise grade a hardware role as a match because it says "engineer".

## Preferences and constraints

These drive the score more than skills do, so be honest rather than aspirational.

- What you optimize for: offer probability, stability, compensation, mission, remote.
- Company types you want, in order. e.g. large enterprise > unicorn > early startup.
- Hard nos: locations, industries, contract types, shift work.
- Anything already applied to, so it can be filtered as a duplicate.
